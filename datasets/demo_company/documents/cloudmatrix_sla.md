# CloudMatrix Service Level Agreement (SLA)

This document outlines the service availability and uptime commitments for the CloudMatrix platform.

## Service Uptime Commitment
CloudMatrix guarantees a minimum monthly uptime commitment of 99.99% for all production instances.

## Service Credit Policy for Outages
In the event of a P1 outage (defined as critical service unavailability affecting more than 20% of active users), service credits will be issued according to the following credit policy:
- For uptime between 99.9% and 99.99%: A 10% credit of the monthly subscription fee.
- For uptime between 99.0% and 99.9%: A 25% credit of the monthly subscription fee.
- For uptime below 99.0%: A 50% credit of the monthly subscription fee.
