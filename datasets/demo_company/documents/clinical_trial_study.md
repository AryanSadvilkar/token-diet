# Clinical Trial Study Report - Phase 3

This document summarizes the outcomes of the Phase 3 clinical evaluation for the investigational compound.

## Primary Efficacy Endpoints
The primary efficacy endpoints observed in Phase 3 trials were:
1. Significant reduction in mean systolic blood pressure (a decrease of 14.2 mmHg from baseline, p < 0.001).
2. 45% reduction in cardiovascular event risk over a 24-month follow-up period compared to the placebo group.
3. Rapid normalization of arterial blood flow within the first 6 weeks of starting treatment.
